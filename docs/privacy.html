Privacy Policy

Checkup for Stardew Valley does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

  • The app has no accounts, analytics, telemetry, or advertising.
  • Save files are read locally and never leave your Mac. The app only reads the Saves folder you point it at (or a file you open), and never modifies save files.
  • Preferences (output settings, the chosen Saves folder) are stored locally in the app's own preferences.
  • Links to the Stardew Valley Wiki open in your browser; the app itself makes no network requests.

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to share with third parties and nothing to request or delete.

Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
